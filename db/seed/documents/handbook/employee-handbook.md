# Employee handbook

This handbook explains how we work day to day. It applies to every employee
and contractor. When a team policy and this handbook disagree, the handbook
wins unless the team policy was approved by the People team.

## Working hours and remote work

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you
choose when to work, as long as your team can reach you for planned meetings.

Everyone may work remotely up to four days a week. Teams agree on one shared
office day so that planning and reviews happen in person. Fully remote
contracts need approval from your manager and the People team.

When you work from abroad for more than ten working days in a calendar year,
tell the People team at least two weeks before you travel. Tax and insurance
rules differ by country, and some client contracts do not allow work from
outside the European Union.

## Leave

### Annual leave

Full-time employees get 26 days of paid annual leave per year, plus public
holidays where they live. Part-time employees get a share based on their
contracted hours. Up to five unused days carry over to the next year and
expire on 31 March.

Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than
three days. Your manager approves or declines within three working days.

### Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. You do not
need a doctor's note for the first three days. From the fourth day, upload a
note to the HR portal. Sick days do not reduce your annual leave.

### Parental leave

Birth parents get 20 weeks of paid leave and other parents get 10 weeks, in
addition to any statutory leave. Leave can be split into up to three blocks
within the child's first two years.

## Expenses

Claim work expenses in the finance portal within 30 days, with a receipt for
anything over 25 euros. Travel must be booked through the travel desk unless
it is cheaper elsewhere by more than 15 percent. Economy class is the default
for flights under six hours.

Meals during business travel are covered up to 60 euros per day. Alcohol is
not reimbursed except at client dinners approved in advance by a director.

## Equipment

You get a laptop, a monitor and a 600 euro home-office budget when you join.
Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours so that the
device can be locked remotely.
