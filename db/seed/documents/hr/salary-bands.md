# Salary bands

Confidential. Shared only with the People team and leadership. Individual
salaries are never stored in this document.

## Bands

Annual base salary ranges for full-time roles in 2026:

- Level 1, engineer: 48,000 to 60,000 euros
- Level 2, senior engineer: 60,000 to 78,000 euros
- Level 3, staff engineer or architect: 78,000 to 98,000 euros
- Level 4, principal or director: 98,000 to 125,000 euros

## Reviews

Salaries are reviewed every April. A move within a band is based on the
annual review. A move to a higher band needs a promotion case approved by two
directors and the People team.

## Offers

New offers should sit in the lower half of the band unless the candidate's
experience clearly matches the upper half. Offers above the band need CFO
approval and are recorded with a reason.
