# Pricing guidelines 2026

Confidential. Shared only with finance and leadership.

## Day rates

Standard day rates for 2026, before discounts:

- Engineer: 820 euros
- Senior engineer: 980 euros
- Architect or delivery lead: 1,150 euros
- Designer: 760 euros

Rates rise by inflation plus one percent each January for existing clients,
with three months' notice.

## Discounts

Account managers may give up to 8 percent for contracts over 250,000 euros.
Discounts between 8 and 15 percent need a director's approval. Anything above
15 percent needs the CFO, and is offered only for strategic accounts.

## Fixed-price projects

Fixed-price quotes add a 20 percent risk buffer to the estimate. The buffer can
drop to 12 percent when the client has signed a detailed specification and we
have delivered a similar project before.

## Minimum margin

No project may be signed below a 28 percent gross margin. The Research Portal
for Northstar Labs was approved at 31 percent and the Fleet Dashboard for
Harbor Logistics at 34 percent.
