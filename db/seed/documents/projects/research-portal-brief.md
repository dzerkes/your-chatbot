# Research Portal project brief

## Summary

Northstar Labs needs one searchable place for its research reports. Today
reports live in shared drives, email and two legacy wikis, and scientists spend
hours finding earlier results. The Research Portal indexes those sources and
gives each team a workspace with saved searches and reading lists.

## Scope

The first release covers report upload and import from the two wikis,
full-text and semantic search, and team workspaces. Single sign-on uses the
client's identity provider. Out of scope for this year: lab instrument data,
external sharing, and mobile apps.

## Timeline

The project started on 15 January 2026 and is planned to finish by
31 December 2026. Milestones:

- April: import from both wikis and search across them.
- July: team workspaces and saved searches.
- October: usage analytics and the migration of the final archive.

## Team and contacts

The delivery lead is Maya Lindqvist. Northstar's product owner is the head of
research operations. Weekly status meetings are on Wednesdays at 14:00
Stockholm time.

## Risks

Report files in the oldest archive use a format the importer cannot read yet.
If conversion is not ready by August, the archive migration moves into the
first quarter of 2027.
