# Data handling policy

This policy says how we classify, store and share information. Breaking it can
breach client contracts and data-protection law, so read it once a year.

## Classification

Every document, dataset and message falls into one of four classes:

- **Public**: approved for anyone, such as published blog posts.
- **Internal**: for employees only, such as this policy and team plans.
- **Confidential**: limited to named groups, such as pricing, salaries and
  client contracts.
- **Restricted**: personal data and client production data. Access is logged
  and reviewed every quarter.

When you are unsure, treat information as confidential and ask the Security
team.

## Storing data

Store work files only in the company drive or approved project tools. Never
keep client data on personal devices or personal cloud accounts. Restricted
data stays in the client's own environment unless the contract explicitly
allows a copy, and copies must be encrypted at rest.

## Sharing data

Share confidential documents by link with named people, not by attachment.
Links to restricted data expire after 14 days. Before you send data to a new
vendor, including AI tools, the Security team must approve the vendor.

Do not paste confidential or restricted data into public AI chat tools. The
internal assistant is approved for internal and confidential data because it
runs inside our environment and respects document permissions.

## Incidents

Report a suspected data leak to security@company.example within one hour, even
if you are not sure. Do not try to investigate on your own and do not delete
evidence. The Security team decides within 24 hours whether a client or a
regulator must be told.
